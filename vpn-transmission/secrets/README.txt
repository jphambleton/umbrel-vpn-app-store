Put your AirVPN WireGuard values here as three plain-text files (NOT committed to git):

  wireguard_private_key    PrivateKey from the AirVPN .conf   e.g. yCbHtKAgJASnJs2S/aZsjag9Fy04csmR4qDUDkJgX2c=
  wireguard_preshared_key  PresharedKey from the .conf
  wireguard_addresses      IPv4 part of Address only          e.g. 10.170.23.45/32

Then restart the app.
